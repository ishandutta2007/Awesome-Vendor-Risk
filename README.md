# Awesome-Vendor-Risk

## Top Vendor Risk Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM), Security Ratings, Vendor Assessments, Continuous Monitoring & Supply-Chain Risk*  
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Vendor Risk / Third-Party Risk Management**. These tools help organizations assess, monitor, and manage cybersecurity, compliance, and operational risks introduced by vendors, suppliers, and other third parties through questionnaires, security ratings, continuous monitoring, and evidence collection.

**Examples** include OneTrust Third-Party Risk, SecurityScorecard, UpGuard, BitSight, Whistic, Vanta, ProcessUnity, Panorays, Black Kite, and Prevalent (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for vendor risk assessment, security scoring, questionnaire management, SOC 2 analysis, supply-chain risk evaluation, and related open tools — ideal for security, GRC, and procurement teams seeking transparent, self-hosted, or complementary alternatives to commercial TPRM platforms.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[OneTrust Third-Party Risk Management](https://www.onetrust.com/)**  
  Enterprise GRC and third-party risk platform offering vendor assessments, continuous monitoring, due diligence workflows, and broad compliance coverage.

- **[SecurityScorecard](https://securityscorecard.com/)**  
  Security ratings platform that continuously scores organizations and their vendors on external security posture with threat intelligence insights.

- **[UpGuard](https://www.upguard.com/)**  
  Vendor risk and breach risk platform combining security ratings, questionnaire automation, continuous monitoring, and evidence workflows.

- **[BitSight](https://www.bitsight.com/)**  
  Cyber risk intelligence and security ratings platform used for portfolio-level third-party risk monitoring and quantification.

- **[Whistic](https://www.whistic.com/)**  
  Vendor assessment network focused on standardized questionnaires, trust profiles, and efficient evidence sharing between customers and vendors.

- **[Vanta](https://www.vanta.com/)**  
  Compliance and trust management platform that includes vendor risk review capabilities within broader security and compliance workflows.

- **[ProcessUnity](https://www.processunity.com/)**  
  Highly configurable enterprise third-party risk management platform supporting complex assessment and remediation processes.

- **[Panorays](https://www.panorays.com/)**  
  Third-party risk platform combining automated security assessments with external attack-surface and posture monitoring.

- **[Black Kite](https://blackkite.com/)**  
  Cyber risk quantification and third-party risk platform that translates security findings into financial risk insights.

- **[Prevalent](https://www.prevalent.net/)**  
  Dedicated third-party risk management solution covering the full vendor lifecycle from onboarding through continuous monitoring and offboarding.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[LT-VRM](https://github.com/learntprm-design/lt-vrm)**  
  Free and open-source Third-Party Risk Management platform supporting vendor onboarding, security questionnaires, risk scoring, contract management, breach monitoring, and a vendor portal.

- **[RiskHub](https://github.com/W1z4rd1c4/RiskHub)**  
  Open-source risk operations platform for managing governed risks, controls, KRIs, vendors, approvals, evidence, and governance workflows.

- **[OpenSSF Scorecard](https://github.com/ossf/scorecard)**  
  Open-source tool from the Open Source Security Foundation that automatically scores software repositories on security best practices and supply-chain risk indicators.

- **[Hipcheck (MITRE)](https://github.com/mitre/hipcheck)**  
  Open-source CLI tool that assesses and scores software packages and repositories for long-term and active supply-chain risk.

- **[SOC 2 Analyzer](https://github.com/ethicalkaps/soc2-analyzer)**  
  Open-source tool that analyzes SOC 2 Type II reports and produces concise risk assessments, exception summaries, and recommended follow-up questions.

- **[TPRM Vendor Scorecard Tools](https://github.com/joseruiz1571/tprm-vendor-scorecard)**  
  Automated open-source utilities that parse SOC 2 reports, map controls to frameworks (NIST, ISO), and generate vendor risk scorecards.

- **[Vendor Due Diligence Tools](https://github.com/YashKutehub/vendor-due-diligence-tool)**  
  Open-source vendor verification and risk-scoring tools that leverage public registries and web signals for basic due diligence.

- **[Security Questionnaire & Assessment Frameworks](https://github.com/)**  
  Community projects and templates for building, distributing, and scoring vendor security questionnaires (SIG, CAIQ, custom).

- **[Supply-Chain Security Tooling](https://github.com/ossf)**  
  Broader OpenSSF and related open-source projects focused on dependency risk, SBOM, and package health evaluation.

- **[GRC & Risk Register Prototypes](https://github.com/)**  
  Open-source risk registers, control libraries, and simple TPRM workflow applications that can be self-hosted.

- **[Continuous Monitoring Helpers](https://github.com/)**  
  Scripts and tools for external attack-surface scanning, certificate monitoring, and basic vendor posture checks.

- **[Evidence & Document Management Components](https://github.com/)**  
  Open-source document vaults and evidence collection tools useful for storing SOC 2 reports, policies, and assessment artifacts.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Full TPRM platforms**: LT-VRM and RiskHub represent the most complete self-hosted open-source efforts in this space.
- **Software supply-chain risk**: OpenSSF Scorecard and Hipcheck for evaluating open-source and package risk.
- **Assessment automation**: SOC 2 analyzers and scorecard generators that accelerate manual review work.
- **Foundational GRC**: Open-source risk and control management tools that can be extended for vendor workflows.
- Many internal and community **vendor risk** and **third-party assessment** projects continue to appear on GitHub.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **LT-VRM or RiskHub** for core vendor workflows, **OpenSSF Scorecard / Hipcheck** for software risk signals, SOC 2 analysis tools for evidence review, and simple questionnaire engines to create a practical self-hosted third-party risk management capability.
